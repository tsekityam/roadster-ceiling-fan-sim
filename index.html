Can fan suction hold a Roadster upside down?

A quantitative model of Tesla's October 2026 McGregor demo: multi-mode fans pull air from under the car, create a pressure drop, and produce downforce F = delta_p * A. Cold-gas thrusters are separate and are not what holds the car on the ceiling.

Simplified steady-flow model for teaching. Press figures are cited; seal geometry and efficiency are labeled assumptions. This does not claim any production Roadster already drives upside down on public roads.

6. Ramp angle (optional sequence)

Along a ramp at angle theta from flat (0 deg) to inverted (180 deg), the normal component of weight that suction must overcome is mg * cos(phi), where phi measures how much of weight presses into the surface. Inverted (theta = 180 deg) is the hardest case: required F >= mg.
